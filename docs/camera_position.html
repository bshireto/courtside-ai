Where to put the camera

Use the same spot every game so the rim and the 3-point arc land in the same place in the frame. That keeps make/miss and 2-vs-3 calls consistent.

Setup 1

Half court (one basket)

WhereBeside the court, level with the free-throw line, 3 m back from the sideline
Height2.5–3 m (pole or fence)
AimAbout 30° toward the basket
LensMain 1x
In frameRim in the upper third, about ⅓ in from the side. Whole arc, both corners, the key and the top of the arc all visible.
Setup 2

Full court

WhereAt the centre line, 4–5 m back from the sideline
HeightAs high as you can, ideally 3 m+
AimStraight across the court, horizon level
LensUltra-wide (0.5x/0.6x). With the 1x lens you'd need to stand around 18 m back to fit both baskets.
In frameBoth baskets and both baselines, with a little margin at each edge

Phone settings and checklist

  • Landscape, 1080p. Use 60 fps if the phone allows (cleaner slow-mo and rim checks).
  • Lock focus and exposure (long-press on the rim, then lock).
  • No zoom during the game, and never move the camera mid-session.
  • Plug into a power bank, and make sure you have at least 10 GB free.
  • Keep the rim against a plain, dark background, not a floodlight.
  • No players or bags between the camera and the rim.
  • Before starting, check the whole 3-point arc is in frame. That's how shots get marked as 2 or 3.
  • If you must move the camera, stop and restart the recording.
